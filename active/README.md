# active/

Business work that is not a single client or lead. One subfolder per topic:

- `active/marketing/` for content and campaigns
- `active/offers/` for pricing and packaging you are designing
- `active/tools/` for internal scripts and automations
- `active/briefs/` for research on prospects that do not have a folder yet

Rules: no loose files at the top of `active/`. Each topic folder can carry its own `README.md` or `HANDOFF.md` if the work spans sessions. `active/.tmp/` is scratch and can be emptied at any time.
